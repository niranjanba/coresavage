Frequently Asked Questions

  • Who can invest in coresavage?

    Any individual over the age of 18 years having a valid PAN and Aadhaar card can invest. The advantages of investing in Coresavage are High return rate upto 21% IRR, pre-agreed monthly income and are based on ownership of an asset.

  • KYC documents required?

    A valid PAN and AADHAAR card.

  • Why we prefer IRR over ROI?

    The internal rate of return (IRR) is a metric used in financial analysis to estimate the profitability of potential investments. IRR is a discount rate that makes the net present value (NPV) of all cash flows equal to zero in a discounted cash flow analysis.
    Whereas, ROI is the absolute return on investment that doesn't hold the time value of money into consideration.

  • What is the Refund policy?

    Any Refund request is applicable only before the purchase of an asset.

  • What happens if the lease is terminatted with partner?

  • How does coresavage charges?

    We charge a total of 2% on your income earned from assets.
